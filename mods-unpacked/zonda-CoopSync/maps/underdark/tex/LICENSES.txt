Rock textures for ULTRA HD (Underdark map). All from ambientCG, licence CC0 1.0 (public domain),
https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ , no attribution required. Downloaded 2026-09-23 as 1K-JPG and
reduced to 512x512 JPG: albedo (desaturated a little so the map's own biome colour leads, contrast
compressed and scaled to the mean linear luminance noted in manifest.json), normal (the pack's NormalGL
map, OpenGL/+Y convention as Godot expects, renormalised after resize), roughness (Drowned x0.55 for wet
stone, Crystal x0.9). 'tint' in manifest.json is a gentle colour cast with luminance 1.0, so
albedo * tint keeps the mean luminance listed.
