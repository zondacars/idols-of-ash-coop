THE UNDERDARK - sound files in this folder (maps/underdark/sfx)

Every .ogg here is derived from a Freesound.org recording released under
Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The licence was checked
on each sound's page on 2026-09-23 (page links to publicdomain/zero/1.0).
Processing (ZondaCoopSync v4.9): downmixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, filtered
(high/low-pass, some pitched down), beds trimmed to 20-60 s with a 2 s equal-power
crossfaded loop seam, loudness-normalised (beds -23 LUFS, oneshots -18 LUFS,
look-ahead limited where needed), encoded Ogg Vorbis. Attribution is not required
by CC0 but is given as a courtesy.

Entries in manifest.json that start with res://sfx/ are the game's own sounds
(Idols of Ash). They are referenced at runtime only and are NOT copied here.

Added in ZondaCoopSync v5.0 (2026-09-24): the omen, idol, Shade, hearth, harrier and stone oneshots
(files named <kind>_<freesoundid>_<n>.ogg). Each licence was checked live on the sound's own page
on 2026-09-24 (the page links to publicdomain/zero/1.0). Same processing: mono, 44.1 kHz, filtered,
-18 LUFS with a -1 dBFS peak ceiling (short transients sit lower; their manifest db adds up to +6),
Ogg Vorbis. The shade_shriek cuts are sped up over their 1.1 s (a rising pitch); stone_groan and
one stone_boom are pitched down. Source audio: the Freesound HQ previews.
