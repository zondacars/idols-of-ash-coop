Idols of Ash - Zonda CoopSync (full-sync online co-op, up to 4 players)
Built for game version 1.41. Every player needs the mod installed.

INSTALL
1. Steam > right-click Idols of Ash > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Copy "override.cfg" and the "mods-unpacked" folder into that folder,
   next to idols_of_ash.exe.
3. Launch the game normally through Steam.

PLAY
1. On the main menu press F2. Type a name and a shared password.
2. One player clicks Host. Everyone else clicks Join with the same password.
3. The HOST picks the level and difficulty (campaign or sandbox).
   Guests follow automatically. Guests should not start levels themselves.
4. Press F2 again to close the panel.

WHAT SYNCS
- Everyone sees each other (body, name, health, rope and hook).
- Centipedes are run by the host. With one centipede it hunts whoever is
  closest; with several, they split up and each takes a different player.
- Health pickups are NOT shared. Each player collects their own.
- Level start, restarts, and the ending trigger are shared.
- Deaths: everyone gets 1 free respawn per run (you come back on solid
  ground next to a living teammate, with 3 s of immunity). Die again and
  you spectate; JUMP switches who you watch. Reaching a checkpoint
  refreshes the team's respawn AND revives anyone who is spectating.
  When the last living player dies, the run restarts for the whole team.

ALL MAPS UNLOCKED
Every sandbox map is unlocked for everyone, without touching campaign
progress or firing Steam achievements.

NEW MAP: INFERNO (sandbox)
A 1100 m custom descent, roughly twice the campaign's depth, in three parts:
  - Hell: red rock, firelight, and traps.
  - The Dark: an 80 m stretch with no light at all. Follow your friends'
    name tags.
  - The Frozen Circle: ice, blizzards, and a frozen lake at the bottom.
Traps to watch for:
  - Sand shelves crumble about a second after you stand on them, and snap
    your rope if your hook is in one. Stone shelves always hold.
  - Fire vents flare on a timer and burn you if you are standing in one.
  - Boulders (and icicles, lower down) drop when you land underneath.
  - Hot updrafts throw your body and your thrown hook.
  - Spike beds on the walls hurt, cut your rope, and shove you off.
  - Ice shelves in the frozen section tilt toward the pit. Keep moving or
    hook in.
  - A second centipede is released once someone passes 330 m down.
Rest platforms every ~95 m are checkpoints: they heal you and bring
spectators back into the run.

NEW MAP: THE UNDERDARK (sandbox), v4 "THE GREAT RIFT"
Rebuilt from scratch around one colossal abyss, modelled on the campaign's
own chasm (measured in game: the campaign is 2,100 m deep and 310-400 m
wide, with 150-300 m sightlines).
  - 3,380 m deep (campaign: 2,100). The rift is 170 to 420 m across, and you
    can see the far wall, the ledges below you and the landmarks from
    hundreds of metres away.
  - 8 strata down the rift plus THE BURROWS and THE NEST:
    THE MOUTH > OSSUARY > THE BURROWS > FUNGAL HOLLOW > ROOTWORKS >
    DROWNED GALLERIES > SUNKEN VILLAGE > CRYSTAL VEINS > THE FOUNDRY (and
    THE CRUCIBLE) > THE NEST
  - Horizontal set pieces that cross the whole void: the Span (a broken
    288 m stone bridge), four giant roots (385-395 m each), two hanging
    village archipelagos (51 platforms on chains), three crystal spars
    (about 390 m each), and the Crucible.
  - Landmarks: THE RIBS (a ribcage the size of a street, grown into the
    Ossuary wall), THE CHANDELIER (18 stalactites hanging under the Lid),
    THE NEEDLES (rock spires standing in the lava lake).
THE FALL RULE (this map only):
  - Fall further than your rope is long (25 m) and you die. No exceptions.
  - Shorter falls still hurt a lot: jumping one balcony costs about a third
    of your health. Let the rope out instead of jumping.
How it plays:
  - You descend along wall balconies, about a full rope apart. 38 of the 101
    drops are LADDERS: small footholds with nothing else below. Deeper down
    the footholds get smaller and shift sideways, so you swing to them.
  - THE PLUNGE (Drowned Galleries): 9 footholds in a row, about 200 m, no
    balcony. There is a checkpoint right before it.
  - On the ladders: sand ledges off to the side RUMBLE AND SHAKE for a
    second, then drop. Rocks lodged in the wall let go when you land under
    them. Below 900 m, centipedes come down the wall after you.
  - THE BURROWS: the rift is plugged by the Lid, the only way on is three
    holes barely taller than you. One goes through (110 m of crawl-tight
    tunnel); the other two are dead ends.
  - Bells (Ossuary gallows, the Nest): throw your hook at one. Anything
    hunting goes to the sound instead of to you for a while.
  - Puzzles: light all 4 kilns in the Fungal Hollow to open the door. In the
    Plate Hall every living climber stands on a plate (alone, the door opens
    for 16 s, so run). Three idol fragments are hidden in side caves and on a
    village rooftop; the Foundry door needs all three.
  - THE CRUCIBLE: 27 iron bars across the whole rift over the lava lake,
    3 places to rest. Hook a bar, swing, throw again. 4 of the bars sink.
  - The Stalker (Ossuary and Drowned Galleries): it only moves while nobody
    is looking at it. Someone always has to watch it. It is very fast.
  - Leave the "Additional Centipedes" slider at 0. The map places its own:
    10 ambushes on the way down, and the Nest wakes when you take the idol.
  - 20 checkpoints. Dying solo reloads you at the team's furthest checkpoint;
    in co-op you respawn next to a teammate as usual.
  - Name tags show through walls on this map. Top right shows biome, depth
    and run time.
  - Ending: take the idol from the altar in the Nest. That wakes the nest and
    opens the sealed tunnel behind the altar. Run for the light.
  - Music and ambience change per biome.
Difficulty: about 8 out of 10. The campaign is about a 5.

ENHANCED GRAPHICS (F4, per player, remembered between sessions)
F4 cycles:  OFF  >  LOW  >  HIGH  >  HIGH + HD
  - LOW:  bumpy stone (normal maps), glow, filmic color, shadows on the 8
          nearest lights.
  - HIGH: LOW + light bouncing off rock (SDFGI), indirect light, reflections,
          shadows on the 20 nearest lights.
  - HD:   HIGH at your screen's full resolution instead of the game's 640x360
          (changes the pixelated look, costs the most FPS).
Works in the campaign and every map. Off by default. The F2 panel shows
FPS and the current graphics mode.

UNINSTALL
Delete override.cfg and the mods-unpacked folder from the game folder.
